Guide

Everything TheRider does, in the order you'll need it. Short version: make a rider, check the finished rider, send it. Tour managers and promoters: add riders to a tour or festival and plan the changeovers.

Venues

What a stage has: size, desks, monitors, mics, backline and power. Check any rider against a venue and see straight away what's there, what to bring and what won't fit. Venue techs can fill in their own venue.

Band & contacts

Who you are and who the venue should call. This goes on the front page of your rider. It opens with example bands: press “+ New rider” to make your own.

Stage plot

Drag gear onto the stage. Grid squares are 1 metre (2 ft in imperial). Click something to name, colour, turn, resize or layer it; arrow keys nudge it.

Lighting plot

Standard plot symbols, lens towards the audience. Shift-click or drag a box to select several fixtures, then group them (front blinders, spots, LED). Your stage plot shows faded underneath.
